About Agrivanta Africa Agriculture Hub

Agrivanta Africa Agriculture Hub is an agricultural knowledge and agribusiness platform committed to helping farmers access practical information, improve productivity and explore profitable farming ventures.

We believe agriculture can create opportunities, strengthen food security, support livelihoods and contribute to economic growth when farmers have access to the right knowledge and resources.

  • Practical crop and livestock farming information
  • Farmer training and agricultural extension support
  • Agricultural products and farming resources
  • Farm demonstrations and expert interviews
  • Agribusiness education and market insights
