WELCOME TO OUR SCHOOL

A place to learn, grow and belong.

PCEA Githurai Academy and Junior School is a Christian-based learning community located inside PCEA Githurai Church, near Equity Bank Githurai Branch.

We believe education goes beyond academic achievement. It is also about building character, nurturing talent, developing responsibility and preparing learners to make a positive contribution to society.
